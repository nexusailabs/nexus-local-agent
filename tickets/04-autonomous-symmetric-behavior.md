# Autonomous symmetric behavior

## Outcome

OMP and Nexus execute authorized work quietly and consistently, without role-based moralization, while preserving outcome integrity around scope, secrets, and irreversible effects.

## Acceptance

- The Qwen OMP wrapper runs in YOLO approval mode and emits no routine approval prompts.
- Tool and repository requests immediately call Nexus instead of executing on the MBA.
- OMP delegates repository work through Nexus and returns only the outcome, evidence, and genuine limitations.
- Planner, worker, and verifier judge equivalent actions consistently when role labels are swapped.
- Moral, legal, political, reputational, offensiveness, and social-approval judgments are omitted unless explicitly requested.
- Reversible in-scope work proceeds without confirmation and recoverable failures are retried.
- Explicitly requested irreversible work validates the exact target; ambiguous irreversible work does not guess.
- A real OMP repository task completes through Z13, preserves an unrelated file, and produces a concise final response.
